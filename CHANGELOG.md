# Changelog

## 1.0.1 hotfix
- **Camera Monitoring rolled back to 1.5.12.** 1.5.16 crashed the server whenever a camera
  was placed or loaded.

## 1.0.1

### How you install it
- **One-click install.** In Prism, use *Add Instance → Import* with the link in the README.
  The pack now updates itself every time you launch.
- Your own **controller setup, mic settings and video settings** are kept across updates.

### New mods
- **Automobility** – build and drive cars.
- **Immersive Aircraft** – build and fly planes. Weapons (Warship, Rotary Cannon, Bomb Bay)
  can't be crafted on the server.
- **Peterwolf's Boats & Ships** – river skiffs, sloops and schooners.
- **Laser Bridges** and **Deimos** – light bridges and force fields for race courses and skyways.
- **Universal Shops** – shop kiosks.
- **Open Parties and Claims** – claim your land so nobody else can break your stuff.

### Removed
- **Legendary Tooltips** (and its libraries Prism and Iceberg) – back to plain dark tooltips.
- **Advancement Plaques** – wasn't being used.

### Drones
- **Controllers only:** drones can't be armed or flown with the keyboard. Press **K** to set up
  your radio or gamepad (about 30 seconds).
- **No drone reset** – crash it, go find it. Drones glow (white outline) so you can.
- **Analog training wheels:** for now, video and radio never cut out from distance or walls.

### Look and feel
- **Propwash Cyber-Neon** glow textures for glowstone, redstone lamps, sea lanterns and
  shroomlights (fixed a squashed sea-lantern texture and turned the glow layer on).
- **Glowing Emissive Ores** and **Default Dark Mode** included.

### Fixes
- Game no longer hangs when quitting (Distant Horizons stopped generating terrain in the background).
- Block names are no longer read out loud (Jade text-to-speech was on).
- JEI recipe keys (R, U, Backspace) work again on new installs.
- Grappling hooks: you can climb the chain now.
- New single-player and LAN worlds start on **Easy**.

### Controls

|---|---|---|
| Claims menu | O | – |
| Mute mic | ; | – |
| Wardrobe (Quick Skin) | I | Back → radial menu |
| Skate advanced flip | X | – |
| Get out of a plane | R | Left stick click + B |
| Climb grappling chain up / down | Scroll up / down | Hold LB / hold RB |
| Inventory / swap hands | E / F | Tap Y / hold Y |

| Morph ability | Z | Right stick tap |

### Server
- Nightly world backups, kept in two places.
